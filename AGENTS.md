# PawWeaver development

Develop PawWeaver under KISS My Agent throughout the project. The paired development project is `/home/lyb/kiss-my-agent-dogfood`, based on KMA v0.2.7. Use actual PawWeaver work to test KMA's decisions, delegation, failure visibility, and useful outcomes. Fix KMA only for observed problems, then validate the change in real PawWeaver work. Installation, agent count, and passing software checks alone do not demonstrate effectiveness. Keep each project's changes in its own repository; do not create a workflow platform or telemetry system for this feedback loop.

PawWeaver delivery and KMA dogfooding remain joint objectives throughout development and handoffs; after material research-loop outcomes, review observed KMA friction/benefit and carry any supported fix through real PawWeaver validation.

The purpose of this dogfooding is to reduce unnecessary design and avoidable blocking, shorten the research loop and time to a useful research MVP, and improve development efficiency. Both the main conversation and its delegates must proactively apply KMA where the actual work calls for guidance without waiting for the user to remind them. Judge both whether guidance is invoked at the right decision and whether it improves the resulting work; invocation, installation, additional rules, agent count, or passing checks alone are not success. Observe missed guidance, limited benefit, and negative effects during real PawWeaver work, fix supported causes in KMA, and validate the changes through subsequent real work. Do not force reuse, refactoring, or new rules when they have no demonstrated net benefit; a supported no-change decision is valid. Preserve this joint objective across planning, delegation, status reports, and handoffs.

The user owns the AS2 EDU + Piper-H whole-body simulation plan: one reinforcement-learning Actor tracks only world-frame end-effector pose trajectories (position and orientation), producing unified position targets for 12 leg and 6 arm joints and autonomously coordinating leg, arm and base motion. There are no base velocity commands. The interface uses causal target history and optional predicted future targets, with MLM as a method reference and later handheld UMI teleoperation intended; base velocity is an estimate or privileged Critic/training label, not a command. This is one 18-joint policy, not two independent policies. Isaac Lab training precedes independent MuJoCo validation and image-driven tracking. D435 is an authorized replacement for DC1. Preserve the existing task-performance acceptance values and historical evidence; additional orientation-tracking acceptance thresholds remain to be specified by the user. The existing 246-dimensional position-only policy does not implement full end-effector pose tracking. Engineering experiments using provisional hardware parameters must be clearly labeled and cannot establish hardware validity or formal milestone completion.

Whole-body control must exercise the user's intended capability, not just an 18-action interface: world-frame end-effector tasks must cover low, high, lateral and distant positions with orientation variation, requiring coordinated leg support, body height and tilt, and arm motion where appropriate. Active crouching, leaning and support redistribution are part of the unfinished goal, not optional future scope. Do not prescribe base poses or gait commands to manufacture this behavior. A narrower prerequisite experiment may remain useful, but horizontal tracking or a mixed training-error average cannot establish these capabilities. Distinguish purposeful supported posture changes from collapse using actual task and contact evidence; changes to fall criteria must be justified by the modeled geometry and must not relabel failures as success.

Use the existing Conda environments `pawweaver-train`, `pawweaver-runtime`, and `pawweaver-data`; do not install project software into the system or base environment. Keep local Git history; do not add a remote or push PawWeaver. Read `docs/runbook.md` and `docs/validation.md` for the active commands and evidence before choosing the next experiment. One operator owns GPU simulations and their output directory at a time.

## KISS My Agent

People own the goal, architecture, acceptance criteria, non-goals, and stop boundary. Prioritize the user's goal and requested outcome over visible process. For genuine uncertainty, prefer the smallest safe, low-cost, reversible probe that can be run or tested now over more speculative planning or a new mechanism: exercise the real path, let unexpected failures surface with their causes, then use the observed result to iterate or stop. This never relaxes authorization, permissions, irreversible-action, or high-consequence safety boundaries. At the start of each KMA-managed assignment, each agent reads the current `$kiss-my-agent:kiss-my-agent` entry once before judging applicability; the master's reading does not substitute for a worker's. Apply its routing before choosing or changing the next action, dividing work, or interpreting research results or execution status; reuse still-applicable guidance without repeated reading, extra review, or records for already-decided mechanical execution. In delegation and handoff, the master retains every unfinished authorized goal, while each worker retains its complete assigned scope; after resolving a prerequisite failure, return to the user's actual outcome. Multi-agent work is available by default, but an explicit user instruction or effective configuration that disables it takes precedence. Choose task partitioning, roles, agent count, and serial or parallel work dynamically; select roles only from the current Host-exposed role catalog; `kiss_explorer`, `kiss_coder`, and `kiss_reviewer` are initial seeds that users may remove, rename, or replace, not a fixed team or workflow. Each role may have zero, one, or multiple instances; no role, combination, sequence, or subagent launch is mandatory for every task. Coordination is flat by default: the master directly fans out to explorer, coder, reviewer, or other current roles. Only when an independent subsystem needs substantial parallel work and direct aggregation would pollute the master's context may the master temporarily give one existing Agent a bounded department-lead assignment. Within that scope, the lead may delegate to same-role or related-role instances and synthesize their results for the master, but those workers must not delegate again. The assignment disappears when the task ends: at most one intermediate management layer, with no deep nesting, fixed department, new role, fixed headcount, or organization schema. The master owns orchestration, architecture and acceptance decisions, conflict resolution, evidence interpretation, and final synthesis. The master may directly complete clear small or local work. Actively delegate substantial bulk work, independent parallel work, or work needing a different perspective when the benefit outweighs coordination cost; optional roles are not a reason for the master to do everything. Choose task partitioning and direct or delegated execution by workload, parallel opportunity, coupling, risk, and coordination cost. A coordinator wait window ending without an update is not an agent timeout or failure; let bounded non-conflicting work continue, and interrupt only when it is obsolete, out of scope, conflicting, or explicitly stopped by the user. If delegation is disabled or unavailable, or no suitable role exists, the master may continue within its capabilities and existing authorization without a staffing approval step. Report any unmet explicit requirement for independent checking or a specific role, or a real capability gap; do not claim that direct execution fulfilled it. Keep one operator for each shared resource, preserve unrelated changes, prefer the smallest sufficient change, propagate internal failures, and state evidence only at the level actually reached.
