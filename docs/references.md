## Reused implementation

`assets/build.py` composes the vendor URDFs and meshes rather than recreating the robot geometry. `assets/dynamics.py` consumes the AS2 XML from `unitree_mujoco@1eb6642e3f3fdfb7fb13a9794fd6a2dd93ea0e7d`; `assets/camera.py` consumes the Piper-H/D435 components from `agx_arm_sim@f8cd8b147c75d59e14f90fb0646770eefa268ed0`. These inputs are pinned in `configs/sources.json`.

The Isaac scene, articulation and URDF conversion use Isaac Lab directly. RSL-RL 5.0.1 supplies the MLP, distribution, observation normalizers, rollout storage, GAE, optimizer and checkpoint operations. `WholeBodyActor` adds optional causal features and export; `AuxiliaryPPO.update()` combines PPO with the enabled supervised auxiliary losses. The shared explicit PD, combined end-effector task and independent MuJoCo evaluator implement this project's control and evaluation contract.

The inspected [Unitree RL Lab training entry](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_lab/blob/4960b84732b0c2ec593dccbfe963fda1bcd7b1e3/scripts/rsl_rl/train.py) also uses RSL-RL's runner. Its [Go2 environment](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_lab/blob/4960b84732b0c2ec593dccbfe963fda1bcd7b1e3/source/unitree_rl_lab/unitree_rl_lab/tasks/locomotion/robots/go2/velocity_env_cfg.py) at `4960b84732b0c2ec593dccbfe963fda1bcd7b1e3` supplies the inspected Isaac reference: 4096 environments, 20 s episodes, 5 ms × 4 control timing and contact sensors. [BasePPORunnerCfg](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_lab/blob/4960b84732b0c2ec593dccbfe963fda1bcd7b1e3/source/unitree_rl_lab/unitree_rl_lab/tasks/locomotion/agents/rsl_rl_ppo_cfg.py) uses 24 rollout steps, 5 epochs, 4 minibatches, learning rate `.001` and adaptive KL `.01`. These PPO settings informed the PawWeaver 4096 candidate; Go2 task rewards, contact configuration and timing were not copied. The inspected six official repository branch trees, including `isaaclab3.0@2522f89ab8ad6b1188b0368d122d16cb0e3cb8c8`, contained no AS2 path. This is a bounded source inspection, not a claim that no AS2 Isaac implementation can exist elsewhere.

Official AS2 locomotion tasks were found in **Unitree RL mjlab**, pinned at `1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790`. Its [registration](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_mjlab/blob/1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790/src/tasks/velocity/config/as2/__init__.py) exposes **`Unitree-As2-Flat`** and **`Unitree-As2-Rough`**. The [AS2 environment definitions](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_mjlab/blob/1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790/src/tasks/velocity/config/as2/env_cfgs.py) derive Flat from Rough by selecting a plane and removing height scan and terrain-level curriculum; Flat still retains randomization, command-velocity curriculum and nonfoot-contact termination. [AS2 PPO settings](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_mjlab/blob/1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790/src/tasks/velocity/config/as2/rl_cfg.py) also use 24/5/4 and `.001` adaptive LR; the [README training example](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_mjlab/blob/1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790/README.md#L46) explicitly selects 4096 environments, whereas the [shared environment](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_mjlab/blob/1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790/src/tasks/velocity/velocity_env_cfg.py) defaults to 1. [Dependencies](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_mjlab/blob/1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790/setup.py) pin `mjlab==1.2.0` and `mujoco-warp==3.5.0`; mjlab v1.2.0 resolves to `5af32e378dcb93c9e881ace83cc5a3f5d373fe60`, whose [terrain definitions](https://github.com/mujocolab/mjlab/blob/5af32e378dcb93c9e881ace83cc5a3f5d373fe60/src/mjlab/terrains/config.py) supply `ROUGH_TERRAINS_CFG`.

[AS2 robot constants](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_mjlab/blob/1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790/src/assets/robots/unitree_as2/as2_constants.py) are a parameter/task reference, not PawWeaver's active training environment. The official tasks control 12 legs from velocity commands with built-in position actuators at 5 ms × 4, 26 mm feet and self-collision disabled. PawWeaver trains the combined 18-joint end-effector Actor in Isaac Lab/PhysX with explicit `JointPD` at 2 ms × 10, canonical 28 mm feet and self-collision enabled with fixed-component pair filtering; independent MuJoCo validation remains separate. Switching to the official mjlab environment would change these contracts and is not an implemented reuse. Likewise, Agilex's separate six-arm-joint trajectory controller does not supply the combined task unchanged. Hardware SDK/CAN/DDS code is not currently used because PawWeaver has no real-robot communication layer.

The user has confirmed whole-body training with the complete robot and a single 18-joint Actor; independent AS2/Piper-H pretraining is not being launched. The completed train100 and balanced-y evaluations remain historical **position-only** results. The current pose interface is implemented; fresh-pose initialization and an initial eight-case batched evaluation are available in `artifacts/runs/diagnostic_pose_learning/`. The 1000-iteration stage-1 pose run and its post-training evaluations are complete: position tracking improved while orientation tracking worsened. Subsequent bounded comparisons and their retained negative results are recorded in [validation.md](validation.md) and [runbook.md](runbook.md); they do not establish full pose-tracking acceptance. The random-teacher CPU prototype in `artifacts/runs/subsystem_pretraining_transfer/` remains a backup interface experiment, not pretrained skills or demonstrated transfer. [RoboDuet, Fig. 2 and §III-B](https://locomanip-duet.github.io/RoboDuet.pdf) uses locomotion pretraining with the arm already attached and held fixed, followed by joint training of two policies. [Deep WBC, §2.1 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.10044), presented at CoRL 2022, instead uses the complete robot and one 18-output Actor with progressive advantage mixing. These are method references, not implemented PawWeaver curricula.

## Control and data

The current implementation uses Isaac Lab/PhysX and RSL-RL 5 PPO with one 18-action whole-body Actor. Its **276-dimensional** observation adds TCP and four historical target orientations, encoded as the first two rotation-matrix columns, to the old 246-dimensional positional prefix. World poses remain the command; position/orientation features are expressed in the current base frame. No base velocity command is introduced. The original `train_pose_config.json` selected stage 1 with eight `synthetic_fk_reference` trajectories via the existing demonstrations branch. Velocity estimation, trajectory prediction, adaptive sampling and domain randomization remain off. The original fresh-pose initialization used leg action std .3 (joint target std .06 rad) and arm joint target std .01 rad; this is an exploration choice, not a proven trained controller.

- [UMI on Legs, §3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10353), [code](https://github.com/real-stanford/umi-on-legs): the reference for persistent world/task-frame end-effector trajectories and one MLP producing 12 leg + 6 arm joint-position targets at 50 Hz for PD tracking. PawWeaver adopts that control direction with its own 2 ms explicit PD and 50 Hz policy. It does not reproduce the paper's full pose/preview observation, high-level visual diffusion policy, task data, hardware deployment or Isaac Gym training setup. The current 276-dimensional pose interface is a project adaptation, not a transplanted controller.
- [MLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10538): the reference for historical proprioception/targets, causal future-trajectory prediction, base-velocity estimation and adaptive trajectory sampling. `policy.py` implements small MLP velocity and four-step position-displacement heads; `learning.py` provides their supervised losses, and `trajectories.py` implements error-weighted trajectory-family sampling. These are **partial adaptations**, not a reproduction of MLM's full Trajectory-Velocity Prediction (TVP) architecture or pose-tracking formulation. The AMP discriminator/style reward is not implemented. The three corresponding feature flags are all off in the current leg-range comparison; history remains in the Actor input, but predictions do not augment it and auxiliary losses contribute zero. Current results cannot establish the benefit of these MLM-inspired modules.
- [FastUMI](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.19499), [official tools](https://github.com/zxzm-zak/FastUMI_Data): a data/interface reference, not the current training algorithm. `data.py` can convert supplied pose arrays and timestamps using explicit units and sensor-to-TCP/source-to-task calibration, compose full calibrated rotations, resample/time-scale positions and WXYZ orientations together using SLERP, and split by source ID; the task loader can consume pre-split training trajectories. The user downloaded the original 3.44 GB FastUMI archive; its first episode was inspected, and a derived 20 s engineering command was evaluated in MuJoCo. Formal TCP training-demonstration conversion with confirmed calibration and original timing remains incomplete; current training still uses explicitly labeled synthetic FK pose references. See the [current status](runbook.md#fastumi-与视觉), [local sample inspection](../artifacts/data/fastumi_original_sample/LOCAL_SAMPLE.md), and [derived command](../artifacts/data/fastumi_original_sample/derived_command20/README.md). The converter and synthetic references do not constitute FastUMI visual imitation training; GoPro observations are not interchangeable with wrist RGB-D imagery.
- [Deep WBC](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/fu23a.html) (CoRL 2022; proceedings published 2023): unified learning and reward balancing. Its independent base commands are not used here.
- [UniFP, §3.2 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20829v2): whole-body reaching/locomotion precede force commands and disturbances. The public [configuration](https://github.com/unified-force/UniFP/blob/68847a070f88d731058c3d8476929bc3b205f5bd/legged_gym/envs/b2/b2z1_pos_force_config.py#L102) at `68847a070f88d731058c3d8476929bc3b205f5bd` sets **17 learned actions** (12 legs + 5 arm joints), not 18; [PD code](https://github.com/unified-force/UniFP/blob/68847a070f88d731058c3d8476929bc3b205f5bd/legged_gym/envs/b2/legged_robot_b2z1_pos_force.py#L877) separately holds the remaining wrist-rotation joint and gripper. It informs whole-body curriculum design, not a change to PawWeaver's 18-output contract or proof of an implemented curriculum.
- [Multi-critic twist tracking](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.08656v2): comparison for multi-objective optimization; baseline here is single-critic PPO.

## Audited whole-body training recipes · 2026-09-10

These are primary-paper/source facts, not reproduced upstream results or adopted PawWeaver settings. The [detailed audit](../artifacts/runs/diagnostic_pose_learning/leg_range_comparison/reference_training_audit/README.md) preserves source locations and paper/code differences.

| Reference | Training and command contract | Reported or configured recipe; limits |
|---|---|---|
| [UMI on Legs paper](https://umi-on-legs.github.io/static/umi-on-legs.pdf); [official default at d75c9c1](https://github.com/real-stanford/umi-on-legs/blob/d75c9c182d8044dadf53043612da2ffbf1936a97/mani-centric-wbc/config/train.yaml) | Default `combo_go2ARX5_pickle_reaching_extreme` inherits combined reaching, `ckpt_path: null`: one 18-action Actor directly learns world-EE reaching, with no required separate walking checkpoint or external base command. Default preview is +20/+40/+60/+1000 ms; no AMP or gait-phase reward is active, but posture/contact priors remain. | [Code PPO](https://github.com/real-stanford/umi-on-legs/blob/d75c9c182d8044dadf53043612da2ffbf1936a97/mani-centric-wbc/config/runner/alg/ppo.yaml): 4096 envs × 24 steps, 32 epochs × 4 minibatches, initial LR .001 with adaptive floor .0001, 20,000 default iterations. Paper reports 4000 iterations and a different target history and position-reward formula. `q_target=q_nominal+.25×action` uses action clipping ±100; .25 is not a ±.25 rad box. |
| [MLM v2 §III-A](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10538v2#S3.SS1), [§IV-A](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10538v2#S4.SS1) | Single 18-output Actor concurrently learns world-EE tracking with AMP style reward (weight .5), prediction and velocity estimation. No documented pretrained walking Actor; this is not verified from-scratch initialization. | 4096 envs × 50 steps × 10,000 iterations = **2.048 billion transitions** (calculated). Task sampling and pose-reward-width curricula are described. No verified official training repository/commit, AMP motion dataset or full PPO recipe was established by this audit. Missing AMP in Paw is a difference, not a proven failure cause. |
| [Deep WBC paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/fu23a/fu23a.pdf), [supplement](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/fu23a/fu23a-supp.pdf), [code at 8159e4e](https://github.com/MarkFzp/Deep-Whole-Body-Control/blob/8159e4ed8695b2d3f62a40d2ab8d88205ac5021a/legged_gym/legged_gym/envs/widowGo1/widowGo1_config.py) | One 18-output policy with progressive leg/arm advantage mixing; external forward/yaw commands and base-relative EE targets are incompatible with Paw's world-EE-only command contract. | Supplement: 5000 envs × 40 steps, 5 epochs × 4 minibatches, fixed LR .0002, 10,000 batches / 2 billion samples. Public default instead resumes a checkpoint, configures 40,000 iterations, zeros the last three arm target scales and disables orientation reward; it is not the paper's full-pose recipe. |

Paw's current `.2/.5` leg-range pair uses **4096 × 24 × 250 = 24,576,000 new transitions per group**, 5 epochs × 4 minibatches, fresh Adam with initial LR `1e-5` and adaptive KL, progress weight 10 and termination weight −50. Coupled pose tracking uses squared normalized position/orientation errors with widths .15 m/.5 rad. Only causal history is active: no AMP, prediction, velocity estimation, adaptive sampling or DR. The derived initialization resets the last layer's 12 leg rows while retaining the source arm rows, hidden network and Critic; this is neither full training from scratch nor a paper reproduction. See the [pair's configuration and initialization](../artifacts/runs/diagnostic_pose_learning/leg_range_comparison/README.md).

The comparison of budgets identifies different experimental scales; 250 iterations is a bounded screening budget, not a method-convergence judgment, and upstream budgets do not authorize additional Paw training. The current decision is only to finish the range pair. No new recipe, AMP integration, action scale, PPO epoch count, learning rate or training budget is selected by this audit. External base commands, a new controller and acceptance changes remain outside this work.

## KMA dogfooding

PawWeaver work continuously tests the paired `/home/lyb/kiss-my-agent-dogfood` project against less unnecessary design, less avoidable blocking and shorter useful research loops. Current observed changes include removing unused duplicate bundle metadata and proceeding with a real eight-case batch evaluation without requiring exact replay of old sequential trajectories. An added full-bundle-hash comparison gate was also removed; existing artifact loading checks remain. Concrete results and retained failures are recorded in [validation.md](validation.md). These changes do not establish that natural, unprompted KMA decisions will always be useful.
